NOVA WORLD — CREATOR CREDIT BUILD
Created by Chidera Bright Nwaokoru

This is a major gameplay-polish build of NOVA WORLD, a global life simulation prototype.

Included in this build:
- 3D explorable NOVA world with roads, waterfront, skyline, stadium, airport and residential districts.
- Enterable homes, market, cafe, lounge, dealership, bank, immigration office and land office interiors.
- Character customization foundation with male/female selection, skin, outfit, hair and simple facial features.
- Walking with analog movement, keyboard movement, sprint/run and jump.
- Mobile controls for portrait and landscape layouts.
- Vehicles with enter/exit, improved headlights, night headlight beams and horn controls.
- Live device-clock day/evening/night atmosphere with street lights.
- Dynamic weather foundation including rain, clouds and fog.
- Tokyo-specific purple/sakura-style falling leaves and a distinct purple evening palette.
- Cold-city snow particle styling for Toronto/London.
- Lagos plus Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu and Kano travel destinations, alongside major international destinations.
- NOVA Cash, work/career systems, land and home ownership foundation, businesses and vehicle ownership.
- Phone systems for messages, bank, life, maps, world, cars, settings and profile.
- NOVA LIVE WebSocket client foundation for real cross-player chat, presence, positions, transfers and voice notes when a server URL is deployed.
- Calm optional ambient audio foundation.
- PWA manifest/service worker support.

IMPORTANT ONLINE NOTE
The included server folder is a development foundation. The browser build does not become truly multiplayer until a real NOVA WebSocket server is deployed and its URL is placed in config.js. Do not describe the offline/local prototype as having real cross-player messaging, transfers or online counts until that server is deployed.

The project is intentionally still In Development so the world can continue expanding.
